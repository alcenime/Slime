✦ OVERVIEW

What is Slime Runes?

Slime Runes is an evolving onchain fantasy universe being built on Robinhood Chain. Adventurers will explore a world of floating islands and ancient ruins, collect powerful runes, and battle monsters, with progress permanently recorded onchain as new gameplay is introduced.

Unlike traditional games, items and progress recorded onchain are truly owned by players — they live onchain and can never be taken away.

A portion of future ecosystem revenue may be allocated toward $RUNE buybacks, subject to the development and growth of the Slime Runes ecosystem. This is a future ecosystem mechanism, not a token allocation, and no guaranteed amount, percentage, or revenue is promised.
