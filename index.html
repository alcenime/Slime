✦ OVERVIEW

What is Slime Runes?

Slime Runes is a fantasy on-chain adventure game built on the Base network. Players choose a unique adventurer, explore a world of floating islands and ancient ruins, collect powerful runes, and battle monsters — all with progress permanently recorded on the blockchain.

Unlike traditional games, your items, progress, and achievements are truly yours. They live on-chain and can never be taken away.
